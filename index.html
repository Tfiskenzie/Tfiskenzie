02 / About / Beginnings

From curiosity to systems thinking.

It started with building simple websites, then evolved into design systems, then into understanding how brands communicate and behave online.

I studied Computer Science at Anchor University. My work sits between development, design, and culture — shaped by music, fashion, photography, and internet behavior.

Over time, I’ve worked on event platforms, e-commerce systems, digital campaigns, and creative brand experiences. Each project is an attempt to make systems feel more human.

I care about clarity, intention, and building digital products that can evolve beyond trends.
