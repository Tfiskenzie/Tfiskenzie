02 / About / Beginnings

It started with curiosity.

First it was websites. Then motion. Then storytelling. Then the realization that every brand — even the loud ones — are usually trying to solve a communication problem.

I studied Computer Science at Anchor University. Somewhere between development, visual direction, photography, gaming communities, and internet culture, my work stopped feeling strictly technical. It became editorial. Strategic. Cinematic.

Over the years I’ve worked across event systems, digital transformation projects, e-commerce experiences, music campaigns, fashion concepts, and creative marketing. Sometimes as the developer. Sometimes as the strategist. Sometimes as the person asking the uncomfortable questions.

I care about interfaces that feel human. Brands that sound like themselves. And systems built to survive scale — not just impress during launch week.
